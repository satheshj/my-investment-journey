# My Investment Journey

This context defines the language used to describe the author's investment history, evolving strategy, and published reflections. It keeps actual financial ownership distinct from plans, research, and interpretation.

## Portfolio facts

**Instrument**:
An investable security or fund, such as NVIDIA, VOO, or UTI Nifty 50 Index Fund. An instrument has an identity independent of whether the author owns it.
_Avoid_: Asset, stock when the instrument may be an ETF or mutual fund

**Holding**:
The author's actual ownership of an instrument during a period. A holding exists only when ownership is supported by real portfolio data.
_Avoid_: Asset, investment idea, planned investment

**Portfolio**:
The author's collection of holdings considered together.
_Avoid_: Account, watchlist

**Portfolio Snapshot**:
A dated record of the portfolio and its values at a particular point in time. It does not imply how the portfolio reached that state.
_Avoid_: Portfolio history, transaction history

**Holding Snapshot**:
The recorded quantity, cost basis, market value, and strategy classification of one holding within a Portfolio Snapshot.
_Avoid_: Asset record, transaction

**Transaction**:
A real executed event that changes ownership of an instrument, such as a buy or sell. A transaction must not be inferred when the source only provides a snapshot.
_Avoid_: Decision, portfolio update

**Valuation**:
The monetary value assigned to a holding at a stated date and currency basis.
_Avoid_: Live price, return

## Strategy

**Strategy Bucket**:
The author's classification of a Holding at a particular time as Core, Thematic, or Experimental. The classification may change without changing the Instrument.
_Avoid_: Instrument type, asset class

**Core**:
A Strategy Bucket for broad-market exposure intended to form the durable foundation of the portfolio.
_Avoid_: Safe, guaranteed

**Thematic**:
A Strategy Bucket for intentional exposure to a specific long-term idea, sector, or economic theme.
_Avoid_: Core, research interest

**Experimental**:
A Strategy Bucket for ownership undertaken while testing an idea or learning through a deliberately limited commitment.
_Avoid_: Mistake, speculative by default

**Investment Plan**:
A possible future allocation or change the author is considering. It does not represent ownership or a promise to transact.
_Avoid_: Holding, current strategy, upcoming purchase

**Research Interest**:
A subject the author is actively exploring without implying an intention to buy.
_Avoid_: Investment Plan, recommendation, Holding

## Journal

**Investment Decision**:
A dated choice or judgment about an Instrument, Holding, or broader strategy, captured with the author's knowledge and expectations at that time.
_Avoid_: Transaction, recommendation

**Reflection**:
A dated reassessment of an Investment Decision or investing experience after the author has learned or observed more.
_Avoid_: Edited decision, hindsight correction

**Learning**:
A genuine insight developed through the author's investing experience or research and presented without claiming universal authority.
_Avoid_: Rule, advice, lesson manufactured for content

**Investment Story**:
A curated narrative that connects financial facts, Investment Decisions, and Reflections without merging or rewriting their sources.
_Avoid_: Brokerage record, recommendation

## Build narrative

**Build Log**:
The internal engineering record of a real project milestone, including decisions, implementation, verification, and learning.
_Avoid_: Raw transcript, command history

**Public Build Story**:
A curated account of selected engineering work prepared for visitors to the website.
_Avoid_: Build Log, raw AI conversation
