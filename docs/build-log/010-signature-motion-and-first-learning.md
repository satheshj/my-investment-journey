# Signature motion and first learning

## Outcome

The homepage now has two deliberate visual-intensity peaks instead of relying mainly
on one-time reveal motion. The Learnings route also publishes its first genuine note,
captured from the author's current beginner-investor framework.

## Motion audit

The earlier motion pass correctly preserved natural scrolling, reduced-motion access,
and mobile reading order, but it stopped at fade-ups and light scrub sequences. It did
not yet deliver the portfolio and strategy transformations promised by the product and
design documents.

The revised motion uses:

- a pinned portfolio heading while the verified allocation composition builds from the
  committed public snapshot;
- a pinned strategy illustration while experimentation, individual stocks,
  diversification, and core-plus-themes advance beside it;
- restrained image-scale progress rather than rotation, blur, or decorative physics;
- normal static flow below the desktop breakpoint and whenever reduced motion is set.

`gpt-taste` informed the pinning and scrub architecture. The Spade scroll references
informed the use of sustained visual states and generous quiet space. The project's
canonical warm editorial design system continued to control typography, color,
density, and accessibility.

## Published learning

The first note records:

- finance-video influences, including Madurai Veeran and Zerodha;
- monthly consistency as the immediate goal;
- a current preference for mutual funds and ETFs over new individual-stock picks;
- the influence of low-cost index investing without presenting it as a guarantee;
- open questions about Nifty 50 versus broader large- and mid-cap exposure;
- space and defence as research interests rather than holdings;
- no current allocation to gold and no invented conclusion about its future role;
- a more careful distinction between emergency liquidity and idle long-term cash.

The note is dated when it was captured. It does not invent the dates of earlier
decisions.

## Evidence boundary

External claims link to primary sources from Berkshire Hathaway, IN-SPACe, and NSE
Indices. Personal beliefs remain written in first person, and plans and research remain
visually distinct from current holdings.
